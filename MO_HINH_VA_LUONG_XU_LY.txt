MO HINH PHAT HIEN DEEPFAKE AUDIO-VISUAL
========================================

1. Muc dich cua mo hinh
------------------------

Day la mo hinh phat hien deepfake audio-visual, tap trung vao:

- Chuyen dong cua vung mieng.
- Dac trung hinh anh cua moi trong tung doan video.
- Dac trung am thanh tuong ung.
- Muc do dong bo giua khau hinh va tieng noi.

Mo hinh khong tao video gia. Mo hinh chia video thanh nhieu doan dai 1
giay, sau do tinh xac suat doan do la FAKE:

    P(FAKE) >= 0.5  -> FAKE
    P(FAKE) <  0.5  -> REAL

Diem tong ket cua video la trung binh P(FAKE) cua cac doan hop le.


2. Cac model thanh phan
-----------------------

2.1. YuNet

File:
    models\face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx

YuNet la model phat hien khuon mat, khong phai model phan loai
deepfake. YuNet co nhiem vu:

- Tim bounding box cua khuon mat.
- Xac dinh vi tri tuong doi cua mieng.
- Ho tro tao vung crop mieng khi MediaPipe khong lay duoc landmark.

2.2. MediaPipe Face Landmarker

MediaPipe lay cac landmark tren khuon mat, trong do he thong su dung
40 diem xung quanh moi de xac dinh chinh xac vung mieng.

Neu MediaPipe khong phat hien duoc landmark hop le, he thong fallback
sang vi tri mieng do YuNet cung cap.

2.3. Model phan loai deepfake

Checkpoint:
    model_best_epoch23.pth.tar
    hoac checkpoint epoch50 da duoc huan luyen

Day la model chinh dung de phan tich dong bo hinh anh va am thanh.
Kien truc gom:

- Nhanh hinh anh ResNet18 2D/3D.
- Nhanh am thanh CNN 1D.
- Co che attention.
- Khoang cach Audio-Visual.
- Lop Linear va Sigmoid tao P(FAKE).


3. Dau vao cua model
---------------------

Moi doan xu ly dai dung 1 giay.

3.1. Dau vao hinh anh

- 30 frame.
- Toc do 30 FPS.
- Moi frame crop vung mieng.
- Resize ve 224 x 224 pixel.
- Tensor co shape:

      (1, 1, 3, 30, 224, 224)

Y nghia:

- 1 mau trong batch.
- 1 doan.
- 3 kenh mau RGB.
- 30 frame theo thoi gian.
- Kich thuoc anh 224 x 224.

3.2. Dau vao am thanh

- 1 giay am thanh.
- Mono.
- Sample rate 48000 Hz.
- PCM int16.
- Tensor co shape:

      (1, 48000)

Neu audio ngan hon 48000 mau thi duoc pad bang so 0. Neu dai hon thi
bi cat ve dung 48000 mau.


4. Tien xu ly video
--------------------

Buoc 1: Doc thong tin video

He thong kiem tra:

- Chieu rong va chieu cao.
- FPS.
- So luong frame.
- Thoi luong.
- Stream video.
- Stream audio.

Video khong co audio se bi tu choi vi model can ca hinh anh va am thanh.

Buoc 2: Chuan hoa kich thuoc frame

Neu canh ngan cua video lon hon 540 pixel, frame trung gian duoc thu
nho sao cho canh ngan xap xi 540 pixel. Viec nay giup giam bo nho va
thoi gian xu ly nhung van giu du thong tin de phat hien khuon mat.

Buoc 3: Chia video thanh cac doan 1 giay

Video duoc doc lai o 30 FPS va chia thanh cac doan:

    0-1 giay, 1-2 giay, 2-3 giay, ...

Mac dinh he thong quet toi da 12 giay dau va dung toi da 10 doan hop le.


5. Loc chuyen canh
-------------------

Moi doan 1 giay duoc kiem tra chuyen canh truoc khi dua vao model:

1. Thu nho cac frame.
2. Chuyen frame tu BGR sang HSV.
3. Tinh histogram mau voi kich thuoc 16 x 8.
4. So sanh histogram giua cac frame lien tiep bang khoang cach
   Bhattacharyya.
5. Neu gia tri lon nhat vuot 0.45, doan bi loai.

Muc dich la khong dua cac doan cat canh manh, ghep canh hoac khong lien
mach vao model.


6. Phat hien khuon mat
-----------------------

YuNet duoc chay tren 5 frame dai dien:

    frame 0, 7, 15, 22, 29

Mot doan chi hop le khi phat hien duoc khuon mat tren it nhat 60% so
frame mau, tuc la it nhat 3 tren 5 frame.

Khi co nhieu khuon mat:

- O frame dau, chon khuon mat lon nhat.
- O cac frame sau, chon khuon mat gan vi tri truoc do nhat.

Cach nay giup theo doi cung mot nguoi va han che chuyen sang nguoi khac.


7. Crop vung mieng
-------------------

Che do mac dinh la mouth_lm.

Voi moi frame mau:

1. MediaPipe lay 40 diem landmark quanh moi.
2. Chuyen toa do landmark tu dang chuan hoa sang toa do pixel.
3. Tinh hinh chu nhat bao quanh moi.
4. Tao mot o vuong bao quanh vung mieng.

Kich thuoc o crop co dang:

    max(1.6 x chieu rong moi, 50% canh khuon mat)

Viec dat kich thuoc toi thieu bang 50% canh khuon mat giup khong crop
qua chat khi nguoi trong video dang chu moi hoac noi nho.

Neu MediaPipe that bai:

    mouth_lm -> mouth

He thong fallback sang crop dua tren tam mieng va kich thuoc khuon mat
do YuNet cung cap.


8. On dinh vung crop
--------------------

Sau khi co cac box mieng tren 5 frame mau:

1. Hop nhat cac box.
2. Tao mot crop box duy nhat cho toan bo 30 frame.
3. Mo rong crop voi he so MOUTH_MARGIN = 1.1.
4. Ap dung cung mot o vuong cho ca 30 frame.
5. Resize moi crop ve 224 x 224.

Neu vung mieng di chuyen qua nhieu, doan bi loai. Dieu kien:

    MAX_UNION_RATIO = 2.0

Muc dich la tranh crop bi nhay vi tri hoac chon nham nguoi.


9. Chuan hoa hinh anh
---------------------

Moi frame duoc xu ly nhu sau:

1. Chuyen tu BGR sang RGB.
2. Chuyen sang tensor.
3. Chuan hoa voi:

      mean = (0.5, 0.5, 0.5)
      std  = (0.5, 0.5, 0.5)

Cong thuc:

    pixel_normalized = (pixel / 255 - 0.5) / 0.5

Gia tri dau vao hinh anh nam xap xi trong khoang [-1, 1].


10. Tien xu ly am thanh
-----------------------

Audio duoc tach bang FFmpeg voi cau hinh:

- Mono.
- Sample rate 48000 Hz.
- PCM 16-bit.

Sau do audio duoc chuan hoa min-max:

    audio_norm = (audio - min) / (max - min + epsilon)

Va dua ve khoang xap xi [-1, 1]:

    audio_input = (audio_norm - 0.5) / 0.5

Audio im lang van duoc xu ly an toan nho epsilon trong phep chia.


11. Kien truc ben trong model
-----------------------------

11.1. Nhanh hinh anh

Nhanh hinh anh su dung ResNet18 2D/3D de hoc:

- Dac trung khong gian cua vung mieng.
- Chuyen dong cua moi qua 30 frame.
- Bien doi cua khau hinh theo thoi gian.

Model khong chi nhin mot frame, ma phan tich ca chuoi 30 frame lien tiep.

11.2. Nhanh am thanh

Audio di qua CNN 1D gom:

- Conv1D kernel lon de trich xuat dac trung ban dau.
- BatchNorm.
- ReLU.
- MaxPool.
- Cac lop Conv1D tiep theo.

Dac trung am thanh sau do duoc bien doi sang khong gian dac trung tuong
ung voi hinh anh.

11.3. So sanh Audio-Visual

Model tinh sai khac giua dac trung video va dac trung audio:

    (video_feature - audio_feature)^2

Sau do tao ban do khoang cach Audio-Visual. Khoang cach nay the hien
muc do khop giua tieng noi va chuyen dong mieng.

Co che attention tap trung vao cac vi tri va thoi diem quan trong.
Checkpoint epoch50 su dung:

    with_attention = True
    residual_conn = False

Cuoi cung, ban do khoang cach duoc dua qua:

    Linear -> Sigmoid

de tao ra xac suat P(FAKE) trong khoang [0, 1].

13. Cach tinh ket qua cuoi cung
--------------------------------

Moi doan hop le cho ra:

- P(FAKE).
- A-V distance.
- Nhan REAL hoac FAKE.

Diem toan video:

    overall_score = mean(P(FAKE) cua cac doan hop le)

Voi nguong mac dinh 0.5:

    overall_score >= 0.5 -> FAKE
    overall_score <  0.5 -> REAL

Cac doan bi loai do chuyen canh, khong co mat, thieu frame hoac mat di
chuyen qua nhieu khong duoc dua vao phep tinh trung binh.


14. Tom tat
----------

    YuNet
      -> Tim khuon mat va vi tri mieng

    MediaPipe
      -> Tim 40 diem landmark quanh moi

    ResNet18 3D
      -> Hoc chuyen dong mieng qua 30 frame

    CNN am thanh
      -> Hoc dac trung tieng noi

    Attention
      -> Tap trung vao vung va thoi diem quan trong

    Audio-Visual distance
      -> Do muc do khong dong bo giua hinh va tieng

    Sigmoid
      -> Tao xac suat FAKE

Day la mo hinh phat hien deepfake dua tren su bat thuong cua khau hinh
va su dong bo audio-visual, khong chi dua tren mau sac cua mot frame
don le.


15. De xuat thi nghiem: crop toan mat va crop moi
--------------------------------------------------

Muc tieu la do anh huong cua vung anh dau vao doi voi cung mot mo hinh FGI,
giu nguyen nhanh audio. Hai cau hinh can duoc huan luyen rieng:

    A. Face crop: YuNet phat hien mat, mo rong bounding box theo FACE_MARGIN.
    B. Mouth crop: YuNet + MediaPipe landmark moi, dung LIP_SCALE va
       fallback theo YuNet neu landmark khong hop le.

Khong the dung checkpoint da train voi mouth crop de suy luan tren face crop
roi xem do la phep so sanh hop le. Hinh dang va noi dung dau vao model da thay
doi; moi cau hinh can checkpoint duoc train tu dau hoac tu cung mot checkpoint
pretrained, theo cung quy tac khoi tao.

De so sanh:

1. Dung cung danh sach video va nhan DFDC cho train, validation va test.
   Tao split mot lan, khong de cac ban sao/nguon goc cung video hoac cung
   nguoi noi roi vao nhieu split neu co the nhan dien duoc.
2. Giu nguyen audio, kien truc, so epoch, optimizer, learning rate,
   augmentation, batch size va quy tac chon checkpoint; chi thay vung crop.
3. Ap dung cung seed cho cap chay tuong ung. Tot hon la lap lai voi nhieu seed
   va bao cao trung binh cung do lech chuan.
4. Chon nguong tren validation; chi danh gia cuoi cung tren test da khoa.
5. Bao cao AUC, F1, precision, recall, confusion matrix va so mau hop le/bi
   bo qua cho moi cau hinh. Neu tap lech lop, bao cao them PR-AUC va balanced
   accuracy.
6. Neu co dieu kien, danh gia them tren dataset ngoai ma khong fine-tune hoac
   chinh nguong tren dataset do; ghi ro quy tac quy doi nhan.

Thiet ke toi thieu la hai checkpoint: FGI-mouth va FGI-face, duoc train rieng
tren cung split DFDC. De ket luan chac hon, nen co it nhat 3 seed cho moi
cau hinh va bao cao mean +/- standard deviation. Chi so cua checkpoint hien
co the duoc dung lam moc tham khao; neu quy trinh huan luyen/split khac thi
khong nen dat canh truc tiep nhu mot phep ablation co kiem soat.
